# Medicare Advantage Quality Bonus Revenue Reconciliation

Reconcile quality-bonus and rebate revenue to rating results, enrollment geography, and CMS payment mechanics.

**Primary buyer:** Medicare Advantage plans. **Evidence:** Star measure results, enrollment, county benchmarks, rebate percentages, quality bonus factors, payment reports, and CMS correspondence.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Contract and segment registry
- Star measure ingestion
- Cut-point version control
- Enrollment month reconciliation
- County benchmark mapping
- Quality bonus factor calculation
- Rebate percentage validation
- Double-bonus county logic
- New-plan treatment
- Low-enrollment plan treatment
- Payment report ingestion
- Expected revenue calculation
- CMS variance case workflow
- Revenue true-up ledger
- Contract quality economics
